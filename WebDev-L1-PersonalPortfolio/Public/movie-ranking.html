Best Movies According to Andrew

My Top 3 Movies and Series of All-Time.

Movies

  1. Spiderman: No Way Home

    2. This is the best spiderman movie of Tom Holland, it shows his breaking away from the shadow of Ironman.

  2. Gifted

    3. This is a movie about a young girl who is a math prodigy, and her uncle who is trying to raise her. It shows the struggles of raising a child with special needs.

  3. Real Steel

    4. This is a movie about robot fighting handled by humans and the dynamic relationship formed between a robot(Atom) and a young boy Max who just lost his mom.

Series

  1. Stranger Things

    2. This is a series about a group of kids who are trying to find their friend who has gone missing. It shows the struggles of growing up and the dangers of the unknown.

  2. Dong Yi

    3. This is a South Korean historical drama series about a young woman who becomes a concubine in the Joseon Dynasty. It shows the struggles of navigating a complex social hierarchy and the dangers of the unknown.

  3. Avatar: The Last Airbender

    4. This is an animated series about a young boy who can control all four elements (Air, Water, Earth, Fire) and must save his world from an oppressive Fire Nation empire. It shows the struggles of growing up and the importance of friendship.
