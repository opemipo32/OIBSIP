Our Story

Founded in 2023, built for students

OpeNest Hostels was founded in 2023 with one goal: give students quality, affordable housing in a serene environment. We're constantly innovating and striving to improve — because the place you live should make school easier, not harder.

Every hostel we build is designed around three things students actually ask for: privacy, reliability, and a fair price.

Our services
